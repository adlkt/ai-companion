# AI Companion Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

AI Companion does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

## Data access

- When you explicitly choose the popup-window action, the extension reads the active tab URL so it can open the same HTTP(S) page in a separate Chrome window.
- The extension does not read, scrape, analyze, or store the contents of the active page.
- The Doubao side panel loads the Doubao website directly. Your use of Doubao is governed by Doubao's own terms and privacy policy.
- Login sessions, cookies, conversations, uploads, and other data handled by third-party AI websites remain between your browser and those websites. AI Companion does not receive or store that data.

## Data storage and transmission

AI Companion does not operate a backend service and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not transmit user data to the project maintainers.

## Browser permissions

- `activeTab` is used only after a user action to obtain the current tab URL for the separate-window feature.
- `sidePanel` is used to display the extension's Chrome Side Panel.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be published in this repository and included in the relevant release notes.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open a GitHub Issue in this project's repository. Do not include private account information, cookies, tokens, or other secrets in a public Issue.
